# Research Agent

Cited answers to analyst questions about SEC-reporting companies: reported results, calculations over them, guidance, management commentary, recent developments, and comparison tables across companies or periods.

## Reading an answer

- **Every statement ends with its citations**, e.g. `[1] [3]`. Click a citation to open its source, at the quoted passage where the browser can find it; hover over it to see the verbatim quote the statement relies on.
- **Sources** are listed at the end with links, the publication or filing date, and whether each is primary (an SEC filing or the company) or secondary (news or a third party).
- **The Evidence panel** (on the right; reopen it from the link under the sources) shows every source of the answer with the quotes behind each statement, and each calculation with its inputs and the expression computed in code. Answers are listed newest first.
- **Interpreted as** says how anything the question left open was read (period, metric, company).
- **Not available** lists what was asked but can't be given (not reported yet, not disclosed, not an SEC filer), instead of an estimate.
- The steps above each answer show the companies identified, the research plan, and how many statements were verified or withheld.

## Conversation

Follow-ups can refer to earlier answers ("and the prior quarter?", "compare that with its closest peer"). Each follow-up is rewritten into a standalone question, shown as **Researched as**, before it is researched. When a question is ambiguous, the agent asks; just answer in the chat.

## Settings

- **As-of date:** answer as of an earlier date (point-in-time research). Empty means today.
- **Live web search:** off answers from SEC data and previously cached web results only, without spending Tavily credits.

Out of scope: investment advice, price targets, share prices, analyst consensus and estimates.

## About

Filings and financial data come from SEC EDGAR, web search from the Tavily API, and the language model runs on Nebius.
